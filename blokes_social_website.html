It’s not always easy being a bloke. Financial, career, political, environmental, social, family, identity …and hence mental pressure are all sky high these days. We don’t have the answers to much of this but we know that going it alone is harder than it is together.

Blokes is a community of men of all ages and backgrounds navigating modern life together. We create space for honest conversation, good advice, and genuine mateship without any political, religious or financial agenda.

# Community Aims

  • • Shoulder-to-Shoulder Mateship: Connecting through casual, real-world activities (dinners, casual sports, outdoors, running, cycling, pub hangs) without awkward icebreakers.
  • • Open to All Blokes: Expanding beyond specific niches (like dads-only) to embrace men of all ages and life stages.
  • • Low Noise, High Value: Dedicated channels so members only get notifications for activities and topics they care about.
  • • Mental Health Through Activity: Providing organic support networks and alcohol-free/casual options to catch up and unwind.

# Membership Pledge

  • • Respect & Inclusivity: Welcome every bloke regardless of background, career, age, or beliefs. No politics, no preaching, no sales pitches.
  • • Turn Up & Join In: Keep things informal, casual, and low-pressure. Show up when you can, contribute when you want.
  • • Mates Watch Out for Mates: Keep conversations confidential, foster genuine trust, and keep communication noise low and respectful.
