It’s not always easy being a bloke. Financial, career, political, environmental, social, family, identity …and hence mental pressure are all sky high these days. We don’t have the answers to much of this, but we know that going it alone is harder than it is together.

Blokes is a community of men of all ages and backgrounds navigating modern life together. We create space for honest conversation, good advice, and genuine mateship without any political, religious or financial agenda.

# Community Aims & Purpose

  • • Mateship: Bring men of all ages and backgrounds together so no bloke has to face life alone.
  • • Low Pressure, No Agenda: Create space for honest conversation, connection and practical support—free from political, religious, or financial agendas.
  • • IRL Focus: Create community through in-person events of all types.
  • • Generational Leadership: Provide positive role models for future generations of men.

# Membership Pledge & Code of Conduct

  • • Behaviour: Respect for every human is non-negotiable; we stand together against sexism, racism, ageism, hate speech and any form of harassment, abuse or toxic behaviour.
  • • Inclusively Exclusive: All adult blokes are welcome regardless of race, religion, age, background, or education—however being part of Blokes is a privilege, not a right.
  • • Confidentiality: What happens in Blokes stays in Blokes. All discussions and shared personal experiences are governed by Chatham House Rules to maintain a safe, trusting environment.
  • • Disciplinary Process: To protect the safety, confidentiality, and integrity of the group, breaking the membership pledge or disrespecting fellow members will result in immediate removal.
